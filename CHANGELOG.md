# Changelog

## 0.12.0 (2026-11-18)

The first version published as a GitHub release, as a universal macOS zip (signed ad hoc, not
notarized).

### Added

- **Info View:** a strip along the bottom of the editor, like Live's own Info View. Point at any
  control, pad, grid step or panel and it shows what it does and what its settings mean; pads,
  steps and the library status add what they hold now. Elsewhere, a short hint. It replaces the
  tooltips; the editor is 64 px taller to make room.
- The README's screenshot shows the editor with the Info View.

## 0.11.0

Twelve pads, 24 pattern styles in 4/4, 3/4 and 6/8, kits built from your Ableton library or taken
from Ableton's Drum Rack kits, and drag-out to MIDI clips. Not published as a release.
